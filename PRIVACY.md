# ElizAstro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 31, 2026**

## Summary
ElizAstro does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## Data Collection
This app does **not** collect any data. Specifically:
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No data sent to external servers
- No account creation required
- No cookies or web tracking

## Data Storage
All data (birth profiles, chart calculations) is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This data is never transmitted off your device.

## Third-Party Services
This app uses **no** third-party services, APIs, or SDKs.
